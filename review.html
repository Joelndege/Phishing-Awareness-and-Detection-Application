HOW IT WORKS

Simple rules.
Useful explanations.

The demo checks pasted text for signals such as urgent language, credential requests, mismatched sender and link domains, and shortened or unusual URLs. It shows each matched indicator so a reviewer can understand why the message was flagged.

These checks are educational heuristics, not a guarantee of safety. A clean result does not prove that an email is legitimate. Verify unexpected requests through a trusted, separate channel.
